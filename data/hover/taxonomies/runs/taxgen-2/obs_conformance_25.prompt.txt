that the program's final output happened to survive, is still a violation and is
still reported. Repetition across traces is expected and useful: it is how a
recurring problem becomes visible.

Quote the SHORTEST span that shows it. If what is wrong is that something
required is MISSING, you cannot quote it — say what was required, citing the
clause, and where it should have appeared.

Return ONLY JSON:
{"traces": [{"trace_id": "...",
             "findings": [{"what_happened": "...", "where": "<which part>",
                           "quote": "<exact text>" or null,
                           "missing": "<what was required, and where>" or null}]}]}
