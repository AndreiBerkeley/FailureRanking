It is based on pre-classical melodic forms that consist of a series of five or more musical notes upon which a melody is founded.  Yakshagana rāgas have indigenous rāgas and others derived from other forms of music.  Rāgas in yakshagana are closely associated with a set of melodic forms called "mattu".  Yakshagana rāgas have their own "gamaka".  Although yakshagana rāgas share names with rāgas in other Indian music systems, they are compositionally different, with a few exceptions. In Yakshagana tradition, rāgas are associated with different times of the night throughout which Yakshagana is performed.
