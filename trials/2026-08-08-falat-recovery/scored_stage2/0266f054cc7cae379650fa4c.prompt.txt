The following year, on 30 June 1957, Leopold Stokowski conducted it with the London Symphony Orchestra at the Royal Festival Hall, the composer being present in the Royal Box.
Red Garuda | "Red Garuda" for Piano and Orchestra is the second piano concerto by the American composer Peter Lieberson.  The work was commissioned by the Boston Symphony Orchestra and was composed between 1998 and 1999. The piece is dedicated to Peter Serkin and Seiji Ozawa.
