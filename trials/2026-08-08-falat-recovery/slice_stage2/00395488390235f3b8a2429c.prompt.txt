(a) OCCURRENCES: for each finding, list EVERY distinct occurrence of
that failure in the trace — one entry per occurrence, even if the same

"O2", ...), the finding's code, the step number where it occurs, and a
VERBATIM quote from that step proving it (copy exactly; quotes are
machine-verified).

(b) EDGES: for ordered pairs of occurrences (earlier -> later) and for

label each, only where the trace shows it:
- follow_up:    the later step consumed/built on the earlier occurrence's

- error_shift:  the later occurrence transforms the earlier problem into
                a different deviation
- correction:   a later step fixes or invalidates the earlier
                occurrence's deviation (give the fixing step number)
- redundancy:   the later step repeats the earlier one without new
                information

- no_influence: demonstrably independent
Each edge needs: src occurrence id, dst ("OUT" or occurrence id), label,
consume_step (the step where dst consumed src's output; for correction,
the step where the fix landed), and a short verbatim evidence quote.

an influence path (follow_up/error_shift chain) reaching OUT, or an
explicit correction, dead_end, or no_influence edge. Do not leave any
occurrence unaccounted for.
